## What was NOT verified

- **No real hardware. Everything is Chromium at 375×667.** The emulator has
  no iOS suggestion bar, no sheet accessory bar, and `env(safe-area-inset-*)`
  resolves to 0 — so the keyboard line used throughout is a drawn constant,
  not something the browser enforced. **This is exactly why five sprints of
  QA never saw the original bug.** The owner's Thai test on the iPhone SE
  remains the only proof that counts.
- **`100dvh` with the keyboard up on iOS Safari is untested, and the whole
  fix depends on it.** On iOS `100dvh` does not shrink for the keyboard;
  that assumption is what makes the layer work and it was not executed.
- **WebKit was never run.** Whether iOS Safari applies `:focus-visible` to a
  tapped input, and whether the owner's iOS supports `:has()`, is reasoning
  rather than measurement.
- Not on the Pixel 7 Pro, not on a physical iPhone. The logo is crisper in
  headless Chromium at DPR 3 — evidence, not a photograph.
- No screen reader, no forced-colors mode, rates fetch stubbed.
- **No lane attribution table.** Step 8a was added to the template after
  this sprint was copied from it, so nobody was asked which lane found what.
  The measurement starts next sprint.
