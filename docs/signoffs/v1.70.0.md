## Blocker raised, and fixed

The app reported v1.70.0 with `CHANGELOG.md` stopping at 1.69.0. `preflight`
checked that the two version numbers agreed with each other and that no doc
made a stale claim — a version documented nowhere makes no claim at all, so
it passed both. Fixed, and closed mechanically by `preflight` check 6.
